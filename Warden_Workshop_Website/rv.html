RV Revitalization

Bringing your home on wheels back to life — inside and out.

Your RV Deserves a Second Life

RVs take a beating — UV exposure, road vibration, water intrusion, and years of use leave their mark. Tony has spent decades understanding how these vehicles are built and how to restore them properly, not just patch them up.

From a leaky roof to a full interior transformation, Warden's Workshop is one of the few places in Essex County where you'll find RV expertise paired with genuine craftsman quality.

What's Included

Every service delivered with master-level craftsmanship.

Interior Remodeling

Cabinet refacing, flooring replacement, wall panel restoration, upholstery upgrades, and kitchen or bathroom refresh. Transform the interior of your RV into a space you actually want to be in.

Leak Repair & Weatherproofing

Roof sealing, slide-out resealing, window and vent caulking, and structural drying of water-damaged areas. Tony identifies the source of every leak before sealing — not just the symptom.

System Maintenance Checks

Inspection and servicing of propane, plumbing, electrical, and slide-out systems. Catch problems before they become expensive failures — a smart investment before every season on the road.

Transportation Services

Need your RV moved to the workshop or to a storage location? Tony offers reliable transportation services within Essex County and surrounding areas, handling your rig with care.

RV work is highly variable in scope. Contact Tony for an inspection and free estimate. Prices updated as of January 2026.
