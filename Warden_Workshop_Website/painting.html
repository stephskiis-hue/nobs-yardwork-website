What's Included

Every service delivered with master-level craftsmanship.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, and doors painted to a flawless finish. Proper masking, surface prep, and clean edges guaranteed. Choose from any colour — Tony works with all major paint lines.

Exterior Painting

Full exterior painting including siding, fascia, soffits, and trim. Surfaces are cleaned, sanded, and primed before any paint is applied, ensuring a durable finish that stands up to Ontario winters.

Deck Building & Staining

Custom deck construction and professional staining or sealing. Tony selects materials suited to the climate and applies penetrating stains that protect the wood for years of outdoor use.

Fence Installation

Privacy fences, picket fences, and decorative enclosures installed level and square. Wood, vinyl, or composite — installed with proper post-setting to ensure they stay upright season after season.

Power Washing

High-pressure washing for driveways, decks, siding, fences, and patios. Removes years of grime, mildew, and weathering — an essential first step before any painting or staining project.

All pricing is project-specific and based on surface area, prep required, and materials chosen. Contact Tony for a free on-site estimate. Prices updated as of January 2026.
