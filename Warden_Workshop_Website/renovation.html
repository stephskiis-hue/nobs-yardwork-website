Home Renovation

Practical improvements with the quality you won't find anywhere else.

Built to Last, Finished to Impress

Home renovation should improve how you live — not just how things look. Tony approaches every renovation with the same attention to structural integrity and finish quality, whether it's a single drywall patch or a full kitchen refresh.

What's Included

Every service delivered with master-level craftsmanship.

Drywall Repair & Install

From hairline cracks to full panel replacement, Tony delivers seamless drywall work that disappears under paint. New installations are finished level, with proper taping and feathering for a smooth result.

Kitchen & Bathroom Touches

Backsplash installation, cabinet refacing, hardware upgrades, vanity replacement, and tiling — practical updates that dramatically improve the look and function of your kitchen or bathroom.

Flooring Installation

Hardwood, laminate, vinyl plank, and tile — expertly installed with proper subfloor preparation. Tony ensures every floor is level, tight, and finished cleanly at transitions and edges.

Custom Trim & Carpentry

Crown moulding, baseboards, window casings, built-in shelving, and wainscoting. Custom carpentry that adds architectural character — measured, cut, and fitted with precision by hand.
