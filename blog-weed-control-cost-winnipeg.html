Weed control in Winnipeg typically costs $80–$280 per application, with most residential lots landing between $120 and $200 depending on lot size, weed density, and how many treatments your yard needs through the season. That wide range trips up a lot of homeowners who expect a flat rate — here's what actually drives the price, what you should expect to get for it, and how to tell whether a quote is fair.

Average Weed Control Cost in Winnipeg (2026)

Most Winnipeg weed control companies price by square footage, with a minimum call-out fee that typically starts around $80. Here's what the numbers look like by lot size:

  • Small lot (up to 3,000 sq ft): $80–$130 per treatment
  • Medium lot (3,000–6,000 sq ft): $130–$200 per treatment
  • Large lot (6,000–10,000 sq ft): $200–$280 per treatment
  • Acreage or oversized lots: custom pricing

Most Winnipeg yards — a standard 50×100 city lot — fall squarely in the medium range. If a company is quoting you under $80 flat with no other details, ask what's included: some low-cost services apply a weaker formula that only suppresses surface growth without reaching root systems.

Seasonal packages covering 2–4 applications through the growing season usually offer a discount over single-visit pricing. Expect to pay $300–$600 for a full-season plan on an average Winnipeg lot.

What Affects the Price of Weed Control in Winnipeg

Three factors move weed control pricing more than anything else in Winnipeg:

Lot Size and Weed Density

This is the biggest driver. More square footage means more product and more time. A lawn overtaken by dandelions, creeping Charlie, or clover needs heavier product concentration — and often a second application — compared to a yard with scattered weeds. Winnipeg's clay soil tends to hold dandelion seeds through freeze-thaw cycles, so yards with heavy infestations often need extra treatment in the first season before the weed load drops.

Number of Applications

A single spring spray handles active surface weeds. But broadleaf weeds like dandelions in Winnipeg frequently require 2–3 applications spaced 4–6 weeks apart to stay ahead of new germination through the season. Manitoba's short growing season means timing matters — miss the optimal May–June window and you're chasing weeds through July and August instead of getting ahead of them.

Product Type

Selective broadleaf herbicides — the standard choice — target dandelions, clover, and plantain without harming your grass. Non-selective treatments cost less per application but kill everything, so they're typically reserved for driveways or path edges. Companies using licensed commercial-grade products will charge more than consumer-grade services, but the results last noticeably longer and protect your lawn through Manitoba's unpredictable spring weather.

What's Included in a Professional Weed Control Treatment

A professional weed control visit in Winnipeg should include a walkthrough assessment of weed type and density, a calibrated spray application across the full treatment zone, a wait-period recommendation (usually 24–48 hours before watering or foot traffic), and a re-treatment policy if weeds persist within the service window.

Ask any Winnipeg weed control company if they offer a callback visit if weeds re-emerge within 30 days. Reputable services include this — it's a good filter for quality.

How Often Should You Apply Weed Control in Winnipeg?

One application a year isn't enough to stay ahead of dandelions in Winnipeg's prairie climate — especially in heavy clay soil where seeds survive multiple freeze-thaw cycles and re-germinate reliably each spring. Most Winnipeg homeowners see the best results with a two-application approach:

  • Spring (May–June): Targets active dandelions and early broadleaf weeds before they go to seed
  • Late summer (August): Suppresses late-season growth and reduces seed bank going into fall

Yards with heavy weed pressure — especially after the snowmelt in April exposes bare patches — may need three applications to bring a bad year under control. Pairing weed treatments with overseeding thin patches through our spring and fall cleanup packages helps crowd out weeds naturally over time, so you need fewer applications each year.

DIY vs Professional Weed Control in Winnipeg

Box-store weed killers run $25–$60 per bottle and cover roughly 2,000–3,000 sq ft per tank. A professional service costs more upfront, but the math changes quickly once you factor in a few things specific to Winnipeg:

  • Application precision: Over-applying consumer products on Winnipeg's clay soil can damage grass roots — recovery from chemical burn in a clay lawn takes weeks longer than in sandier soils
  • Licensing: Licensed applicators are required for commercial-grade products in Manitoba; consumer products available at hardware stores are diluted versions of professional formulas with shorter residual effect
  • Coverage: A backpack sprayer covers a fraction of a standard Winnipeg lot per tank; professionals treat the full property in one calibrated pass
  • Re-treatment policy: Reputable services guarantee a callback if weeds re-emerge within 30 days — DIY has no such backstop

For one or two isolated weed spots, DIY is fine. For full-lawn coverage in Winnipeg where dandelions spread fast from late April onward, professional treatment is typically more cost-effective after the first season — especially if you're also enrolled in our basic lawn care in Winnipeg and want to keep your maintenance consolidated with one crew.
