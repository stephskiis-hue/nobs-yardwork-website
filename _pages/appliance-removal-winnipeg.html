appliances

The fridge in the garage has been there long enough

An old fridge is 250 lbs, an awkward shape, and illegal to dump — the refrigerant inside has to be recovered by a certified technician before the unit can be scrapped. That's why most people leave it where it is for three years.

We take fridges, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, dishwashers, air conditioners, water heaters and furnaces. Basement, garage, second floor, doesn't matter.

What we do

  • Disconnect water lines and dryer vents, and cap them off
  • Stair-climbing gear for the narrow basement stairs in older Winnipeg homes
  • Floor and doorframe protection on the way out
  • Delivery to a certified recycler, with the refrigerant recovered properly

All metal? Ask about a discount.

If your load is all appliances and scrap, we recover value on it at the yard. That means we may be able to reduce the price — or on a full load, take it for free. It costs nothing to ask.
