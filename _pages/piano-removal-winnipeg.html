heavy & awkward

400 to 800 lbs of cast iron in a wooden box

An upright piano is not a case of getting enough friends together. Almost all of that weight is a single cast iron plate, and the wrong technique on a staircase breaks a foot, a floor, or both.

Same story for safes, pool tables, cast iron tubs, hot water tanks, treadmills and arcade cabinets. If it is heavy, awkward, and the last three companies said no, that is usually why people end up calling us.

What we do

  • Uprights, baby grands and full grands
  • Safes, pool tables, cast iron tubs, treadmills, arcade cabinets
  • Stairs and tight turns — the normal case, not the hard one
  • Piano dollies, straps and enough crew that nobody is improvising
  • Floor and doorframe protection on the way out

What it costs

Quoted on the instrument and the access — how many flights, how tight the turns, how far to the truck. Tell us those three things and we can usually price it over the phone.

Try to rehome it first — realistically

If your piano is genuinely playable and holds a tune, try to rehome it before calling us. But be realistic: most old uprights have no resale value, and schools and churches turn them down routinely. If it has been in a damp basement for fifteen years, it is a removal job, not a donation.
