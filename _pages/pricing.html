the honest part

Why heavy material is priced differently

Here's the part most companies bury in fine print, so we'll just say it:

Concrete, brick, patio stone, asphalt, gravel, dirt, plaster and shingles are priced by weight, not by volume.

The reason is simple: the landfill charges us by the tonne. A half-trailer of couches might weigh 400 lbs. A half-trailer of concrete can weigh over 6,000 lbs. Charging the same for both would mean either overcharging you for the couches or going out of business on the concrete.

So for heavy material we quote by weight, we tell you the rate upfront, and we show you the scale ticket if you want to see it. No BS.

What we don't take

We're licensed and insured, and there are things we legally can't put in our trailer:

  • Wet paint, solvents, chemicals, pesticides
  • Asbestos or suspected asbestos materials
  • Propane tanks, fuel, oil, explosives
  • Medical or biohazard waste
  • Ammunition

Call us anyway — we'll point you to who can handle it. That call is free.

Can I get a discount?

Yes, in three situations:

  • Repeat and contractor accounts — ask about volume rates
  • Existing No-BS Yardwork customers — you already know us, so we take care of you
  • Full-load scrap metal jobs — if it's all metal, we may be able to reduce the price or do it free, because we recover value on it. Ask.
