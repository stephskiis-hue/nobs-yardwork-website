disposal

We don't just bury it

Every load gets sorted before it hits the landfill. Scrap metal goes to the yard. Electronics go to a certified Manitoba e-waste recycler. Good furniture goes to local charities. Fridges and freezers go to a certified recycler who pulls the refrigerant properly — because dumping those is illegal, and we don't do that.

It's better for the city, and honestly, it's how we keep your price down.
