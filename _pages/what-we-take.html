Furniture & mattress removal

Couches, sofa beds, sectionals, dressers, tables, desks, cabinets, carpet. We do the lifting and the stairs. Anything still in good shape gets offered to local charities before it goes anywhere near a landfill.

Mattresses & box springs

Winnipeg curbside pickup won't take these and they're awkward in a car. We bag them on site so nothing sheds through your hallway, and take them to a facility that strips the metal and foam for recycling.

E-waste & electronics

TVs, monitors, computers, printers, satellite dishes and cables. All of it goes to a certified EPRA Manitoba e-waste recycler — not to the dump, where most of it is illegal to put anyway.

Renovation & construction debris

Drywall, lumber, shingles, ceiling tile, plaster, lathe and tile. We can schedule around your build so your trades aren't tripping over debris, and we'll come back as many times as the job needs.

Shed, deck & fence removal

We tear it down and take it away in one visit — one contractor, one invoice. Frozen ground in winter actually helps here: less rutting, less lawn damage, cleaner site restoration come spring.

Yard waste & brush

Branches, brush, sod, leaves and storm damage. This is the crossover with our landscaping side, so it's work our crews have been doing on Winnipeg properties for years. It goes to composting and organics facilities.

Estate, hoarding & full cleanouts

Whole houses, garages, basements and storage units. These are usually hard days for the family involved, so we work at your pace, set aside anything you want kept, and provide photos on completion if you need documentation.

Scrap metal & tires

Appliances, lawnmowers, fencing, bed frames, pipe and rims. If your load is all metal we may be able to cut the price or take it free, because we recover value at the scrap yard. Tires go through the Tire Stewardship Manitoba network.

Pianos & odd items

An upright piano is 400–800 lbs of cast iron in a wooden box, and it is not a two-person job. Neither is a safe, a pool table or a cast iron tub. Bring us the weird one — that's usually why people call.

Garage & basement cleanouts

The accumulation of a decade, gone in a morning. You point at what stays, we take the rest. Indoor work doesn't care what the temperature is, which makes January the cheapest time of year to get on our schedule.
