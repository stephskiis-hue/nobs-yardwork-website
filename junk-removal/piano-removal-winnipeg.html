400 to 800 lbs of cast iron in a wooden box

Piano removal questions

How heavy is a piano really?
An upright runs 400 to 800 lbs, a baby grand 500 to 700, and a full grand can pass 1,000. Almost all of that is a cast iron plate. It is not a case of getting enough friends together — it is a case of the wrong technique breaking a foot or a floor.

Can my piano be donated instead?
Be realistic: most old uprights have no resale or donation value, and schools and churches turn them down routinely. If yours is genuinely playable and in tune, try to rehome it first. If it has been in a damp basement for fifteen years, it is a removal job.

Can you get it down a flight of stairs?
Yes. Stairs are the normal case, not the hard one. Tell us how many flights and how tight the turns are when you book so we send enough people.
