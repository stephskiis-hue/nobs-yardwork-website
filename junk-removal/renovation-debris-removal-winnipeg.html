reno debris

Your trades should not be working around the pile

Drywall, lumber, shingles, ceiling tile, plaster, lathe and tile. Renovation debris accumulates faster than anyone plans for, and once it fills the garage the job slows down for everyone on site.

We schedule around your build rather than the other way round, and on longer jobs we come back as often as the work needs. One call, no bin sitting on the driveway for a month collecting the neighbourhood's garbage.

What we do

  • Drywall, plaster, lathe, lumber, ceiling tile, shingles and tile
  • Repeat pickups scheduled around your trades
  • Loaded by us — your crew keeps working
  • Heavy material (plaster, shingles, tile) priced by weight, told to you upfront
  • Contractor accounts with priority scheduling and net terms

Everything we take in this category

Drywall
Among the most common renovation materials, and heavier per load than people expect. Joint compound in older homes can contain asbestos, so if you are unsure, have it tested before demolition rather than after.Also: Gypsum board, Sheetrock, Gyprock
Lumber
Pull the nails if you have time, but you do not have to. Wood over four inches thick tips at its own rate at Brady Road, which is one reason we sort loads.Also: Wood, Framing lumber, 2x4s, Plywood, OSB, Trim, Baseboards
Pallets
Stacked flat they take a fraction of the space they do thrown in loose, and space is what you pay for.Also: Wood pallets, Skids
Flooring
Pulled up and hauled. Old sheet vinyl and the black mastic under it can contain asbestos in older Winnipeg homes, so test before tearing it out.Also: Laminate, Hardwood flooring, Vinyl flooring, Linoleum, Subfloor
Tile
Heavy for its volume, so larger tile jobs are priced by weight rather than trailer space.Also: Ceramic tile, Porcelain tile, Backsplash
Shingles
Priced by weight. A full roof tear-off runs to several tonnes and suits repeated loads; a garage roof or a partial is exactly our size of job.Also: Roofing, Asphalt shingles, Roof tear-off
Insulation
Bagged before it moves so it does not shed through the house. Loose, grey-brown, pebbly vermiculite can contain asbestos and must be tested first. We cannot take it until it has been.Also: Batt insulation, Fibreglass insulation, Rigid foam insulation
Plaster
Priced by weight. Common in Winnipeg homes built before the 1950s, which is also why it is worth an asbestos test before a wall comes down.Also: Plaster and lathe, Lath, Horsehair plaster
Ceiling tile
Older ceiling tiles are another material worth testing for asbestos before they come down.Also: Drop ceiling, Acoustic tile, Ceiling panels
Doors
Solid doors in good condition are good reuse candidates.Also: Interior doors, Exterior doors, Closet doors, Storm doors
Windows
The glass is wrapped before it is carried.Also: Old windows, Window frames, Storm windows
Kitchen cabinets
Removed and hauled in one visit during a kitchen or bathroom tear-out. Stone countertops are heavy enough to have their own entry under heavy awkward items.Also: Cabinets, Bathroom vanity, Vanity, Laminate countertop
Siding
Aluminium siding and trim go to scrap; vinyl goes to disposal.Also: Vinyl siding, Aluminium siding, Soffit, Fascia
Construction cardboard
The mountain of packaging a renovation leaves behind. Flattened, it takes surprisingly little room.Also: Bulk cardboard, Appliance boxes
Bathtub
Acrylic and fibreglass tubs and shower stalls are light. Cast iron tubs are a very different job and have their own entry under heavy awkward items.Also: Tub, Shower stall, Shower surround, Toilet, Sink
Interior demolition
We do the tear-out as well as the haul-away: non-structural walls, kitchens, bathrooms and basements. Anything load-bearing needs an engineer and a permit first, and in an older home asbestos testing comes before any of it.Also: Demolition, Demo, Kitchen demolition, Bathroom demolition, Basement demolition, Gutting a room
Flood cleanup
Soaked drywall, carpet and furniture out of a flooded basement. Anything contaminated by sewage or significant mould needs a remediation company involved, and we will say so plainly if that is where it has got to.Also: Water damage cleanup, Sewer backup cleanup, Fire cleanup, Disaster cleanup

Asbestos: get it tested first

Older Winnipeg homes can have asbestos in plaster, floor tile and insulation. We cannot take it, and neither can anyone without abatement certification. If there is any doubt, test before demolition starts — we will point you to an abatement contractor rather than guess.

Renovation debris questions

Is this cheaper than renting a bin?
It depends on the job. A bin makes sense if you are producing debris steadily for weeks. We make more sense for a defined pile, a job with no room for a bin on the driveway, or a site where a bin would sit half-empty for a month collecting the neighbourhood's garbage.

Can you come back more than once?
Yes. On longer renovations we schedule repeat pickups around your trades so debris never builds up to the point where people are working around it.

Do you take drywall and plaster?
Yes, both, along with lathe, ceiling tile, lumber, shingles and tile. Plaster and shingles are heavy, so those get priced by weight — same as concrete.

What about asbestos?
We cannot take it. Older Winnipeg homes can have it in plaster, tile and insulation. If there is any doubt, get it tested before demolition starts. We will point you to an abatement contractor rather than guess.
