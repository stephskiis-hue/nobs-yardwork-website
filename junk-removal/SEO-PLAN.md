# What to build next, and why

Researched 2026-09-23. Every factual claim below carries a source. Read
"Verify before publishing" at the bottom before any of this goes on a page.

## The one-line version

Every junk removal company in Winnipeg with location pages targets
**municipalities outside the Perimeter** — Headingley, East/West St. Paul, La
Salle, Selkirk. Nobody owns the **in-city neighbourhoods**, and nobody connects
a neighbourhood to the actual logistics of removing something from it. That is
the gap.

differentiator against the two biggest brands. The genuinely defensible line is
**dump fees and sweep-up itemised in writing**, which Hagemeister does not do.

## Build in this order

### Tier 1 — reference pages nobody has written

1. **`/winnipeg-dump-guide`** — Brady Road fees, hours, the winter switchover,
   the residents-go-to-the-4R-depot rule almost nobody knows, the
   covered-load fine, and a worked "is a DIY dump run worth it?" with real
   drive times. Targets `winnipeg dump fees`, `brady road landfill hours`,
   `dump run cost`.
2. **`/4r-depots-winnipeg`** — all three depots, the **Wednesday closure** at
   Pacific and Panet, residents-only, garbage only at Brady, the full 2026
   accepted list with its exclusions. Then the honest close: here is everything
   they take free, here is what they don't (mattresses, upholstered furniture,
   general garbage, drywall, treated lumber, shingles) — and that is us.
3. **`/hoarding-cleanout-winnipeg`** — a stated differentiator with no page.
   Discretion, unmarked vehicles, room-by-room, keepsakes and documents,
   biohazard limits, multi-day pricing, the caller is usually family.
4. **`/bedbug-furniture-removal-winnipeg`** — verified autocomplete, Super Junk
   competes, and the City's mattress program explicitly excluded infested
   mattresses. Containment protocol, why it cannot be donated, why it goes to
   landfill, how we avoid cross-contaminating the next load.
5. **`/same-day-junk-removal-winnipeg`** — real cut-off times, what makes a job
   same-day-able. Do not claim 24/7 unless it is true.

### Tier 2 — neighbourhood pages, three at a time

**Honest finding: neighbourhood-name search volume is thin.** `junk removal
transcona` autocompletes; `junk removal headingley` and `junk removal sage
creek` return nothing. Build these for map-pack relevance, internal linking and
as something to link from GBP posts — not for direct traffic. **Three excellent
pages beat seventeen thin ones**, which is the doorway pattern Google has
penalised for a decade and exactly what one competitor is doing.

Start with **Headingley, River Heights, Transcona** — they have the most
genuinely distinct content:

- **Headingley** — the best conversion argument on the site. It is a separate
  municipality: **two bags per household per week, extra bags $1 with a yellow
  tag** ([RM of Headingley](https://www.rmofheadingley.ca/p/waste-recycle)),
  and the **4R depots are Winnipeg residents only**
  ([City](https://www.winnipeg.ca/services-programs/recycling-garbage/drop-depots/4r-winnipeg-depots)).
  So a cleanout there essentially must be hauled by a contractor. All three
  national competitors have a Headingley page; none mentions either fact.
- **River Heights** — subdivided 1905–1912, narrow turning staircases, low
  basement headroom, attic conversions with no way down intact, lane-side
  detached garages where the trailer stages, plaster-and-lath demo weight.
  Densest estate-cleanout market in the city.
- **Transcona** — two different jobs under one name: the 1940s–60s railway
  townsite (back lanes, rear garages, narrow lots) versus the new north-end
  subdivisions (no lanes, front-attached garages). **Longest Brady run of any
  area we serve, about 33 minutes each way** — the single best argument against
  a DIY dump run. Recorded **114 mm of rain in the August 2022 storm**, the most
  in Manitoba ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/winnipeg-storm-flooded-basements-1.6552479)),
  so flooded-basement content is legitimately local.

Then St. Vital (50-ft post-war lots, 1950 flood history), Charleswood (deep
lots, ditches instead of curbs, the strongest skid-steer story, Assiniboine
Forest), Sage Creek (built from 2005, no lanes, move-in and first-reno waste,
still building through 2027).

**A neighbourhood page needs all five, and the first three must differ
materially page to page:** the access story, the disposal logistics with that
area's real drive times, why that area calls, the rules specific to it, and a
real photo and review from that postal code. Do not launch one with stock
photography.

Also: the neighbourhood list on the homepage is currently unlinked plain text.
That is where these link from.

## What people actually search

No keyword tool here, so **no volume numbers — any would be invented.** This is
live Google Autocomplete, which proves a query is typed, not how often.

The top modifiers on "junk removal winnipeg" are **prices, kijiji, reddit,
free**. People are price-shopping and cross-checking on forums, which is an
argument for the published card and for a page on what the cheap Kijiji haul
actually costs you.

Strong clusters with **no page at all today**: Brady Road, 4R depots, City
large-item pickup, hoarding, storage unit, demolition, bedbug furniture, fridge
and freezer specifically, couch specifically, same-day, tires, paint disposal.

`junk removal near me` and `same day junk removal` autocomplete but never with
"winnipeg" — those resolve through the map pack, which makes them a **Google
Business Profile play, not a page play**.

## City facts worth quoting (verified)

- **Brady Road**, 1777 Brady Rd. Summer Mon–Fri 5:30am–8pm, Sat–Sun 9–5;
  **winter hours from November 3**. Closed Jan 1, Nov 11, Dec 25. **Effective
  Jan 1 2026: solid waste $99/tonne, yard waste $66, wood $65, clean concrete
  $11.30, clean fill $9, minimum $25 per load.** Asbestos $206/tonne by
  arrangement. Residents are directed to the 4R depot, not the landfill face.
  ([City](https://www.winnipeg.ca/services-programs/recycling-garbage/garbage-disposal/landfill-brady-road-resource-management-facility))
- **4R depots**: Brady 4R (1825 Brady Rd) takes recycling *and* garbage,
  weekdays 8–8, weekends 9–5. Pacific (1120 Pacific Ave) and Panet (429 Panet
  Rd) are recycling only, 10–6 weekdays **closed Wednesdays**, weekends 9–5.
  Free for recyclables; **Winnipeg residents only**.
- **Free at the depots**: batteries, e-waste including TVs and microwaves,
  household hazardous waste *in original packaging with a hazard symbol*
  (including **paint and propane tanks**), automotive fluids, **tires with or
  without rims**, large appliances including fridges and freezers, scrap metal,
  clean lumber, rubble without rebar, branches under 10 cm.
  ([2026 accepted list](https://www.winnipeg.ca/media/3650))
- **Not on that list, and therefore our business**: mattresses, box springs,
  upholstered furniture, general garbage, drywall, treated or painted lumber,
  shingles.
- **Cart collection** refuses overflowing carts, anything beside the cart, and
  all hazardous material. Bulky items need a booked extra pickup: **at least two
  business days ahead, up to three extra bags or ten large items.**

## Verify before publishing

1. **Brady tipping fee contradicts itself on the City's own site** — the depot
   page says $81/tonne, the landfill page says $99/tonne effective Jan 1 2026.
   Call 311.
2. **Mattress drop-off at Brady 4R** was a 2019 pilot and mattresses are absent
   from the 2026 list. Probably ended. Call 311.
3. **City large-item pickup fees, size and weight limits** are not on the
   City's own page; the numbers floating around come from a third-party
   aggregator. Get them from 311.
4. **Drive times** are OSRM routing from neighbourhood centroids, not
   door-to-door. Publish as "about 20 minutes", never as "18.6 km".
5. **Rankings** were not verified from a Winnipeg IP, and there is no map-pack
   visibility here at all.
6. Charleswood "private wells and septic fields" is a single realtor page.
   Check before repeating it.
