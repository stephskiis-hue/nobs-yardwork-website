hot tubs

That hot tub isn't going anywhere on its own

A hot tub is 800 lbs of fibreglass, foam and seized plumbing, usually sitting on a deck behind a fence with a 30-inch gate. It's not a two-person job, and it's not a DIY weekend.

We've pulled them out of tight side yards, off second-storey decks and out of three-season rooms. The tub comes apart on site, goes out in sections, and every piece leaves with us the same visit.

What we do

  • Full disconnect and breakdown on site
  • Cut into haulable sections with no damage to your deck, fence or lawn
  • All debris removed the same visit — shell, cabinet, cover, motor and lines
  • Site swept clean when we leave

What it costs

Price depends on size and access. You get a firm number on a free on-site estimate before anything moves, and it includes labour, hauling and disposal.
