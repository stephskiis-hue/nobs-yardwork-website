The city will not take it and it will not fit in your car

Winnipeg garbage collection does not pick up mattresses or box springs. Left at the curb they just sit there; left in the back lane they earn a bylaw complaint. Meanwhile they are too big for a sedan and too floppy for one person to wrestle.

We bag them on site before they move, so nothing sheds through your hallway, and take them to a facility that pulls the steel springs for scrap and recovers the foam and fibre instead of burying the whole thing.

What we do

  • Bagged on site before the carry, not after
  • Any size — crib, single, queen, king, and the box spring with it
  • Carried down from upstairs bedrooms and out of finished basements
  • Taken for recycling: steel to scrap, foam and fibre recovered
  • Several mattresses in one visit costs less per unit

What it costs

Cheapest as part of a bigger load, but we will do a single mattress on its own. Ask and we will give you the number before we come out.
