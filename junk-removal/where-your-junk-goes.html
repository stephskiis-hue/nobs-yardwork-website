Every junk removal company in this city says "we recycle." Almost none of them tell you what that means. Here's ours.

What you're getting rid of Where it actually goes
Good furniture, appliances, household goods Donated to local Winnipeg charities and reuse centres
Scrap metal, appliances, lawnmowers, fencing Local scrap yards for recovery
Electronics, TVs, monitors, computers, satellite dishes Certified EPRA Manitoba e-waste recycler
Fridges, freezers, AC units Certified appliance recycler — refrigerant is legally required to be recovered by a certified tech, and we make sure it is
Tires Tire Stewardship Manitoba network
Cardboard, paper, books Recycling depots and donation
Concrete, brick, asphalt, aggregate Construction & demolition facility for processing and reuse where possible
Yard waste, brush, branches Composting and organics facilities
Everything genuinely left over Brady Road Resource Management Facility

the honest reason

Why we bother

Straight answer — it's partly because it's right, and partly because it's business. The landfill charges by the tonne. Every tonne we divert to recycling, donation or scrap is a tonne we don't pay to bury. That saving is why our prices work. We'd rather tell you that than pretend it's pure altruism.

Want proof?

Ask for it. On commercial jobs we'll provide scale tickets and disposal documentation as standard.
