We show up with a skid steer

Most junk removal companies show up with a truck and two guys. That's fine for a couch. It's useless for a quarter-acre of overgrown lot, a torn-out parking pad, or 20 tonnes of demo debris.

Skid steer and a weight-rated dump trailer, run by crews who have been clearing properties in this city for years. If another company gave you a no, that's usually a capacity problem, not a physics problem.

What that actually lets us do

Commercial property cleanups

Vacant lots, back alleys, abandoned dumping sites, retail and industrial yards. We scoop, load, haul and leave it clean.

Construction & renovation debris

Drywall, lumber, shingles, ceiling tile, plaster, lathe, concrete and tile. Scheduled around your build so your trades aren't tripping over debris.

Concrete, aggregate & earth

Old sidewalks, patio stones, broken driveways, gravel, dirt and fill. Weight-rated dump trailer, priced by the tonne, scale tickets available.

Full structure demolition

Sheds, decks, fences, garages, hot tubs. We tear it down and take it away in one visit. One contractor, one invoice.

Estate, foreclosure & turnover

Full property clears for realtors, landlords and property managers. Photos on completion so you have documentation for the file.

Snow hauling

When the banks get too high to push any further, we load the snow out and take it off site entirely. Seasonal contracts and per-visit calls both available.
