A cracked driveway is not a wheelbarrow job

Broken sidewalks, sunken patio pads, old front steps, gravel, dirt and fill. This is the work most junk removal companies quietly decline, because a pickup truck can't legally carry it and two people with shovels can't move it.

We show up with a skid steer and a weight-rated dump trailer. We break it out, load it, and take it to a construction and demolition facility for processing. This is the work we were already doing on the landscaping side — the concrete-out, patio-stone-in job is one of our most requested.

What we take

  • Concrete: driveways, sidewalks, pads, steps, footings
  • Brick, patio stone, ceramic tile and asphalt
  • Gravel, dirt, sod, clay and fill
  • Plaster, lathe and shingles

Priced by the tonne, and we'll show you why

Heavy material is quoted by weight, not by trailer space, because the landfill charges us by the tonne. A half-trailer of couches might weigh 400 lbs; a half-trailer of concrete can weigh over 6,000 lbs. We tell you the rate upfront and we'll show you the scale ticket if you want to see it.
