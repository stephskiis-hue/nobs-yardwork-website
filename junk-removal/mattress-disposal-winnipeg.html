Mattress disposal in Winnipeg

  1. Home

mattresses

The city will not take it and it will not fit in your car

Winnipeg garbage collection does not pick up mattresses or box springs. Left at the curb they just sit there; left in the back lane they earn a bylaw complaint. Meanwhile they are too big for a sedan and too floppy for one person to wrestle.

We bag them on site before they move, so nothing sheds through your hallway, and take them to a facility that pulls the steel springs for scrap and recovers the foam and fibre instead of burying the whole thing.

What we do

  • Bagged on site before the carry, not after
  • Any size — crib, single, queen, king, and the box spring with it
  • Carried down from upstairs bedrooms and out of finished basements
  • Taken for recycling: steel to scrap, foam and fibre recovered
  • Several mattresses in one visit costs less per unit

Everything we take in this category

Mattress
City curbside collection will not take them. We bag every mattress on site before it moves, and it goes to Mother Earth Recycling on Main Street, Winnipeg's only mattress recycler, where the steel, foam and fibre are separated.Also: Queen mattress, King mattress, Double mattress, Twin mattress, Single mattress, Pillow-top mattress
Box spring
A box spring is mostly wood and steel, which is exactly what the recycler recovers. It goes with the mattress in the same trip.Also: Boxspring, Foundation, Bed base
Memory foam mattress
Awkward rather than heavy: foam flops and will not stay folded. Bagged like any other.Also: Foam mattress, Latex mattress, Mattress topper
Adjustable bed
The base has motors and wiring, so it goes through electronics and scrap rather than mattress recycling. Hospital beds are heavy and usually come apart first.Also: Adjustable base, Electric bed base, Hospital bed
Crib mattress
Small, but still not something the City will take at the curb.Also: Toddler mattress
Futon mattress
Denser and heavier than it looks. The frame is under Furniture.Also: Futon pad
Waterbed
It has to be fully drained before we can move it, and that is a job for the day before, not the day of.Also: Waterbed mattress
Pillows
Taken as part of a bedroom or full cleanout. Clean bedding in good condition can be donated.Also: Bedding, Duvet, Comforter, Sleeping bag
