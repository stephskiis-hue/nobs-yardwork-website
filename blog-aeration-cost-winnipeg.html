How Much Does Lawn Aeration Cost in Winnipeg?

  2. Blog

Written by The No-BS Team  |  March 30, 2026  |  Winnipeg, MB

If you've been Googling lawn aeration in Winnipeg, you've probably noticed that most companies don't post their prices online. This guide gives you real, current numbers so you can budget properly and know what questions to ask when you call around.

Average Lawn Aeration Cost in Winnipeg (2026)

For core aeration on a standard residential lot in Winnipeg, expect to pay between $80 and $200. The range is wide because lot sizes vary significantly across the city — a small infill lot in the West End is a fraction of the size of a corner lot in Transcona or a newer build in the south end.

Here's a quick reference breakdown:

Lot Size Approx. Lawn Area Typical Aeration Cost
Small (older inner-city) Under 2,000 sq ft $80 – $110
Medium (typical suburban) 2,000 – 4,500 sq ft $110 – $160
Large (corner lot, newer area) 4,500 – 7,000 sq ft $160 – $210
Acreage / estate 7,000+ sq ft Custom quote

These figures reflect professional core aeration using a ride-on or walk-behind aerator with hollow tines — the only type worth paying for. Spike aeration is cheaper but counterproductive for Winnipeg's clay soil. For more on why, see our complete lawn aeration guide.

What Factors Affect the Price?

Beyond lot size, a few things will push your quote higher or lower:

  • Obstacles and tight spaces. Lots with many trees, garden beds, playground equipment, or narrow side yards take longer to aerate properly. Expect a small premium for complex layouts.
  • Soil condition. Extremely dry or hard soil is tougher on equipment and slower to work. If you can water the lawn thoroughly 24–48 hours before aeration, it helps both the results and the price.
  • Time of year. Spring slots (May) are in highest demand. Booking in March or April often locks in the same price while securing a better schedule window.
  • Add-on services. If you add overseeding, power raking, or fertilizing at the same visit, the per-service cost typically drops compared to booking each separately.

Aeration Cost by Lot Size — More Detail

Small Lots (Under 2,000 sq ft of lawn)

Common in older Winnipeg neighbourhoods like Wolseley, St. Boniface, and the West End. Aeration-only on a small lot typically runs $80–$110. The lower cost reflects the shorter time on site, but the per-square-foot rate is often the same as larger properties.

Medium Lots (2,000–4,500 sq ft of lawn)

This is the most common residential size in Winnipeg's established suburbs — areas like St. Vital, Charleswood, and River Heights. Standard aeration runs $110–$160. Most spring cleanup packages targeting this size come in around $250–$350 when power raking and debris removal are included.

Large Lots (4,500–7,000 sq ft of lawn)

Newer developments in the south end, south St. Vital, and Transcona often fall into this range. Aeration alone runs $160–$210. These properties benefit most from bundled packages where the per-service cost is lower.

What's Typically Included in an Aeration Service?

When you book aeration with a professional lawn care company in Winnipeg, here's what a standard service should include:

  • Core aeration of the entire lawn area (plugs left on surface to decompose)
  • Hand-aerating or skip-aerating around obstacles and tight corners
  • Doorstep flag marking for irrigation heads if requested

What's usually NOT included unless specifically quoted: overseeding, fertilizing, power raking, debris cleanup, or lawn edging. Always confirm what's in the price before booking.

Aeration + Overseeding Bundles

Overseeding immediately after aeration is one of the smartest lawn investments you can make. The aeration holes give grass seed direct soil contact, which dramatically improves germination rates compared to seeding on an unprepared surface.

A bundled aeration + overseeding service in Winnipeg typically runs $175–$300 depending on lot size and seed type. Premium grass seed (e.g., a high-quality Kentucky Bluegrass blend suited to Manitoba) costs more but produces better long-term results.

Is Lawn Aeration Worth It in Winnipeg?

For most Winnipeg lawns: yes, absolutely. Winnipeg's clay-heavy soil compacts easily and benefits dramatically from annual or biennial core aeration. A $100–$160 aeration combined with a $50 fertilizer application will outperform $300 in overseeding on a compacted lawn every time — because nothing grows well in soil that's too tight to absorb water and nutrients.

If your lawn is already in good shape and the thatch layer is under half an inch, you may be able to aerate every other year. But if the lawn is thin, patchy, or slow to green up in spring, annual aeration should be a non-negotiable part of your maintenance routine.
