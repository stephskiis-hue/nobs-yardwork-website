How Much Does Lawn Care Cost in Winnipeg?

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Lawn Care Pricing Guide

Written by The No-BS Team  |  March 11, 2026  |  Winnipeg, MB

One of the most common questions we get from Winnipeg homeowners is: "What does lawn care actually cost?" The answer depends on your lot size, what services you need, and how often you want them. This guide breaks down real 2026 pricing so you know exactly what to expect before you call.

Lawn Mowing Prices in Winnipeg

Grass cutting is typically priced per visit based on lot size. Most Winnipeg residential properties fall in the small to medium range.

Lot Size Per Cut (Approx.)
Small lot (under 4,000 sq ft) $45 – $65
Medium lot (4,000 – 7,000 sq ft) $65 – $90
Large lot (7,000+ sq ft) $90 – $140+

Weekly or bi-weekly contracts typically come at a lower per-cut rate than one-off visits. If you're paying over $140 for a standard Winnipeg residential lot, you're likely overpaying.

Lawn Aeration Costs in Winnipeg

Aeration is one of the best investments you can make for your lawn's long-term health. Winnipeg's heavy clay soil compacts significantly over winter, and aeration opens it up for water, air, and nutrients to reach the root zone.

Service Typical Price Range
Core aeration (average lot) $80 – $150
Aeration + overseeding $130 – $220
Aeration + fertilizer application $120 – $200

Spring & Fall Cleanup Pricing

A proper spring or fall cleanup in Winnipeg typically includes power raking, leaf removal, edging, and debris disposal. Pricing varies by property size and how much debris has accumulated.

Cleanup Type Typical Price Range
Basic cleanup (debris removal + rake) $120 – $200
Full spring cleanup (power rake, aerate, edge) $200 – $400
Fall cleanup (leaves, beds, final mow) $150 – $300

Full Season Lawn Care Packages

Many Winnipeg homeowners opt for a full-season maintenance package that bundles mowing, fertilizing, aeration, and seasonal cleanups. These packages offer the best value and take lawn care completely off your plate.

Our basic lawn care package covers regular mowing and basic maintenance, while our premium lawn care package includes a full suite of treatments to keep your lawn thick, green, and weed-free all season.

What Affects the Price?

  • Lot size — the biggest factor in pricing
  • Frequency — weekly is cheaper per visit than bi-weekly or one-off
  • Accessibility — gated yards, slopes, or obstacles add time
  • Condition — overgrown lawns take more work on first visit
  • Add-ons — fertilizing, weed control, edging all add to the base price

Is It Worth Hiring a Lawn Care Company in Winnipeg?

For most homeowners, yes. When you factor in the cost of a quality mower, fuel, maintenance, your time, and the physical effort — professional lawn care often costs less than DIY when calculated honestly. Plus, a professional will catch issues like grubs, thatch buildup, or fungal problems before they become expensive repairs.
