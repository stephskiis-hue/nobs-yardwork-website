cleanouts

Usually a hard day. We work at your pace.

Whole houses, garages, basements and storage units. Most of the time nobody calls us about an estate because they wanted to — they call because someone died, or moved into care, or the house has to be listed by a date that is closer than it should be.

So we do it your way. Anything you flag gets set aside rather than loaded. Paperwork, photographs and small personal items go in one place. If we are unsure about something, it stays out and we ask.

What we do

  • Full house, garage, basement and storage unit clears
  • Anything you want kept set aside, not sorted at speed
  • Paperwork and photographs collected in one place, not binned
  • Donation sorting — usable goods to local charities first
  • Photos on completion for realtors, landlords and out-of-town family

What it costs

Quoted on a walkthrough, in person or over video. Full-property jobs are quoted as a job, not by the hour, so the number does not move because a day ran long.
