Spring Care

Spring Care

A complete spring reset for your lawn, including power raking to remove thatch, core aeration to improve soil health, top dressing for stronger growth, and full leaf bed and debris cleanup to leave your property fresh, clean, and ready for the season.

Followed by near-monthly applications to provide the highest level of weed control.

Fall

Season-End Cleanup

Thorough fall cleanup with complete leaf removal from lawn and garden beds, final cuts and edging, and optional power raking if requested to prepare your lawn for a healthy winter and a strong spring return.

Our end-of-season cleanup leaves your property clean, protected, and fully prepared for winter — no mess, no stress and no spring surprises.
